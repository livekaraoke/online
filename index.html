BOOKING QUESTIONS

What equipment do you provide?

Live Karaoke uses live guitar, looping, microphones and a sound system. Tell us about your venue so we can confirm the setup for your event.

How much space do you need?

We need a clear performance area, room for singers and access to power. Send us your venue details and we can confirm a suitable setup.

How long is a typical Live Karaoke performance?

The length and breaks depend on the event. Include your preferred start and finish times in your enquiry so we can agree a suitable format.

How do song requests work?

Browse the song list, choose a song and join the singer rotation. The host manages the running order during the session.

Do singers need experience?

No. First-time singers are welcome. Pick a song you enjoy and join in at your own pace.

Can people who do not want to sing still take part?

Yes. Enjoy the live music, support the singers and sing along from the audience.

Can Live Karaoke be booked for private events?

Yes. Enquire about weddings, private parties, corporate events and other occasions using the booking form.

Do you travel to venues across Malta?

Enquiries are welcome from venues across Malta. Share the location so we can confirm availability and arrangements.

How far in advance should we book?

Get in touch as soon as you have a date in mind. Availability depends on existing bookings; you can also enquire about a last-minute event.
